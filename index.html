Your favorites and notes live in this browser. Copying your board bundles all of it into a link (or a code) so someone else opens exactly what you see — and they can send one back the same way.

Screen colors are a starting point, not a spec. Nothing on a monitor is color-accurate enough to approve a production color — use this to narrow the shortlist, then have the factory send physical color chips in the real material and sign one as the master sample.
